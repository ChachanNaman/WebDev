REGEX -> 

Regex (short for Regular Expression) is basically a pattern-matching language used inside strings.
It lets you check whether a string follows certain rules, or extract/replace parts of it.

This is the regular expression (rules for valid username):
^ → start of string.
[a-zA-Z0-9_-] → only letters (uppercase/lowercase), digits, _ or -.
{1,15} → length must be between 1 and 15 characters.
$ → end of string.
So: allowed usernames are 1–15 chars, only letters, numbers, _, and -.

ex : 
"naman123" → valid ✅
"hello_world" → valid ✅
"too_long_username_12345" → invalid ❌ (too long)
"hello@user" → invalid ❌ (contains @)
" " → invalid ❌ (empty after trim)
